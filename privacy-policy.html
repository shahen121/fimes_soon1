Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fimes Uploader ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you use our video editing and management app ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your TikTok account to the App, and only after you give your explicit permission, we may access the following information from your TikTok account:

  • Public profile information: your username, display name, and profile picture.
  • Your videos: the videos associated with your account, which you grant us access to for the purposes described below.

We do not collect or access your password, private messages, contact lists, or any information that is not required for the App's core functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above solely for the following purposes:

  • To display your profile information so you can verify the correct account is connected.
  • To list, organize, and manage your videos within the App.
  • To provide editing features (such as trimming and cutting) on your own videos.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App's functionality.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Your information is stored securely and is only used to provide the App's features. We retain your data only for as long as it is needed to provide the services you use. When you disconnect your TikTok account from the App, we stop accessing your account data.

4. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time. To request the removal of your personal information and videos from our systems, please:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account within the App settings, and/or
  • Email us at fimessoon@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including your TikTok username and the email address linked to your account.

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable time (typically within 30 days) and confirm once your data has been removed.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at: fimessoon@gmail.com
